New offers use the supplier's exact network, bundle ID, cost and validity.
Display spelling is corrected without changing the mapped ID. Existing
customer prices are retained if they meet the estimated contribution floor.

Pricing reserves 5.5% of retail for wallet funding, 3% of supplier cost for
supplier top-ups (N30 per N1,000 funded), and the actual model cashback.
The remaining estimated contribution must be at least N30 per purchase,
before hosting, tax and other overhead. Funding in larger batches reduces
the supplier fee allocation. This estimate is not a guaranteed net profit.

All 47 wallet checkout paths, supplier ID payloads, cashback and margins
were simulated in an isolated test database. Failed-payment refunds and
repeat catalogue application were tested. These are not live delivery tests;
no customer numbers were sent to the supplier and no funds were spent.

The migration is a dated snapshot, not an automatic daily supplier sync.
Refresh supplier quotes and review changed costs/availability regularly.
