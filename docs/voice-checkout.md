# Payvict voice checkout

Status: planned for a later release.

- Offer **Talk to Payvict** in the app and a **Voice Purchase** long-press shortcut on compatible Android home-screen installs.
- Start with airtime and data. Recognize Nigerian English and common Pidgin phrases, and offer spoken replies in a Nigerian voice. Test with different accents, phones, and background noise.
- Let customers use a saved recipient or provide a number. Treat the number prefix as a network suggestion and ask the customer to confirm when the network cannot be verified.
- Ask the customer to choose when several bundles match. Show the recipient, network, bundle, validity, and current total. Let customers correct the transcript or type instead.
- Require on-screen confirmation and a transaction PIN before payment. Keep PINs and passwords out of audio and transcripts. Recheck balance, price, and availability on the server and protect against duplicate charges.
- Extend to TV and electricity after the first release, with smartcard and meter verification.
